01About

I'm a Senior Developer and Industry Consultant at IBM Corporation with 13+ years delivering mission-critical systems for Prudential Financial in retirement and insurance. I serve as the UNIX and IBM DB2 Subject Matter Expert — a role built on deep, hands-on ownership of complex enterprise platforms.

Today I architect solutions for the Disability Claim Management System (DCMS), including a CQRS read-replica framework that improves read performance and enables independent scaling of query workloads, and the modularization of a legacy monolith into scalable, fault-isolated service components. I also modernized the DCMS communications layer — integrating SMS and email notifications via Kong-enabled REST APIs with async callback support, replacing legacy batch-based correspondence. I serve as the platform SME for an AIX and IBM DB2 upgrade — owning end-to-end planning, solution design, test strategy, and change management across all affected systems.

I was the SME for Prudential's VISION OmniPlus (Omni) application, where I led its full end-to-end migration from IBM Mainframe (JCL, COBOL, VSAM) to AIX UNIX — one of the most complex platform transitions in the retirement domain. That engagement covered the full migration lifecycle — data extraction, cross-platform migration, and compliance reporting — while managing a batch cycle spanning 20+ upstream and downstream systems with daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual processing runs. I also automated day-to-day operations using Python, Shell scripting, and Excel VBA, and delivered enterprise-wide Knowledge Transfer sessions published on IBM Lighthouse.

Outside of client work, I build developer productivity tools — web apps, VS Code extensions, and CLI utilities — to automate the repetitive parts of daily engineering work. Beyond delivery, I champion AI-augmented engineering through IBM's Innovation Council, integrating tools like Claude and GitHub Copilot to accelerate developer productivity. I hold certifications in AWS (Developer & AI Practitioner), IBM Generative & Agentic AI, and Python (PCAP).
